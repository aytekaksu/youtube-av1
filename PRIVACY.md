# Privacy

The extension runs locally. No analytics, accounts, ads, or server.

It saves your on/off choice and checks the active YouTube tab to offer a reload. It changes AV1 capability answers and cleans up its older forced preference. It does not send browsing data elsewhere.

Access is limited to `www.youtube.com`, `m.youtube.com`, and `www.youtube-nocookie.com`. YouTube's own privacy policy still applies.
