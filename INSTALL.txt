Force YouTube AV1 — Quick start

1. Unzip the download and keep the youtube-av1 folder.
2. Open chrome://extensions (Chrome) or vivaldi://extensions (Vivaldi).
3. Turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and choose that folder.
4. Open a YouTube video. Click the extension icon to turn Prefer AV1 on/off.
5. After a change, click Reload to take effect.

Prefer AV1 starts on. To check: right-click the video, choose Stats for nerds,
and look for av01 under Codecs. If playback stutters, switch Prefer AV1 off.

AV1 may save data or improve quality, but can use more CPU and battery.
